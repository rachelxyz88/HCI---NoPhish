Phishing Scam Using QR Code

Educational Goals

Users learn that phishing attacks have evolved beyond links and emails, QR codes can be weaponized (called quishing) to send victims to fraudulent sites.

What is a QR Code Phishing (Quishing)?

Quishing is a phishing technique where attackers use a QR code to redirect victims to a fake login page or malicious site. Since the URL is hidden inside the QR code, many victims are tricked before they even see the link.

  • Uses logos and designs similar to official emails
  • Uses similar email domains (e.g., @tokoped1a-support.com)
  • Contains urgent messages like “Your account will be blocked in 24 hours!”
  • Fake CTA buttons/links like “Verify Now”

"Phishing Scams Using Fake Rewards and QR Codes Harm Users"

News warns of phishing disguised as hadiah (rewards) using QR codes that redirect users to fake login pages.

Why Do So Many People Get Fooled?

  • People assume QR codes are safe because they’re widely used for payments.
  • Victims don’t see the actual URL until after scanning.
  • The message uses urgency or rewards to lure victims.
The Mindset That Users Should Cultivate: Don’t trust a QR scan automatically, always check the domain shown after scanning and never enter login credentials on a page you didn’t navigate to intentionally
